How is SrednaBG different from Waze?

The two apps complement each other. Here's what each one does:

Capability Waze SrednaBG
Navigates you from A to B, with full turn-by-turn guidance. WazeYes SrednaBGNo
Shows average-speed zones and alerts you on entry and exit. WazePartial* SrednaBGYes
Calculates your live average speed inside a zone and keeps you informed. WazeNo SrednaBGYes

* Waze shows these zones but merges chained checks into one.
