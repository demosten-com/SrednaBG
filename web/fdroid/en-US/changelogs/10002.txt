v1.0.2 — Initial public F-Droid release.

• Real-time running average speed inside Bulgaria's section-control camera zones.
• "Max now" indicator — the top speed you can drive for the rest of the zone without going over.
• Color-coded status (green / amber / red).
• Voice alerts in Bulgarian and English.
• Fully offline: Bulgaria map and zone list bundled in the app.
• Phone-only release. Android Auto support is in progress.
