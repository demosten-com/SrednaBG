v1.1.0

• New: floating overlay over Waze & Google Maps — an opt-in badge shows your live status on top of other navigation apps (off by default; enable in Settings).
• New: per-vehicle speed limits — choose car, truck, bus, or motorcycle and zone limits adjust to match.
• Clearer voice alerts: speeds are spoken more naturally in Bulgarian and English.
• More accurate zone tracking, with fewer false entering/exiting flips at zone edges.
• More reliable GPS for trustworthy speed readings.
• Home and Settings now stay in portrait when your phone is mounted.
• Settings now shows the date and version of the bundled zone list.
• Plus a map screen fix and smaller stability improvements.
